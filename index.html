Wiseshelf

A quiet reader for your own EPUB and FB2 books on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Read your way

Themes with paper textures, your own fonts, fine layout control and status rows at the screen edges.

Your library, not a store

Open your own files. The catalogue shows your iCloud Drive folder by author and series.

AI help without spoilers

The story so far, characters, places and terms, built only from the pages you have read.

Words at hand

Hints above rare words, word cards and review with spaced flash cards.

Highlights and notes

Five colours, a notebook for every book, export to Markdown.

Private by design

No account, no server, no advertising. Sync goes through your own iCloud.

Ваша библиотека, а не магазин

Помощь AI без спойлеров

Слова под рукой

Подсказки над редкими словами, карточки слов и повторение с интервалами.

Выделения и заметки

Приватность
